BEYOND THE STARS

Beyond the Stars was created by me and Lisa Eriksson for our creative programming course.

Inspired by space and black holes, we wanted to create the feeling of being pulled into a black hole and transported through different universes.

The installation consist of five digital worlds that visitors can explore through body movements. By moving closer to the camera on either the lef or right side, visitors travel between worlds, creating an immersive and interactive experience.
